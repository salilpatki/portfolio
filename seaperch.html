SeaPerch Underwater ROV

CAD · FDM · SLA · CFD (OpenFOAM) · Systems Design

🏆 4th of 9,000+ teams worldwide · Innovation Award

Overview

The SeaPerch ROV is a fully custom underwater remotely operated vehicle designed for a global competition. I led the mechanical design and hydrodynamic optimization, engineering the buoyancy, propulsion, and vision systems from concept to a competition-winning build.

The Challenge

The ROV needed to be precisely maneuverable underwater while maintaining stable depth and completing navigation tasks — all within strict size and material constraints. The key engineering problems: achieving neutral buoyancy, minimizing drag, and generating efficient thrust.

My Contributions

Ballasting System

Designed a custom ballasting system to achieve neutral buoyancy and stable depth control, balancing displacement against component weight through iterative testing.

Venturi-Effect Jet Propulsion

Engineered a venturi-based propulsion approach to improve thrust efficiency and maneuverability compared to standard thruster layouts.

Vision System

Integrated a vision system for underwater navigation and task completion, giving the operator reliable situational awareness.

CFD & Hydrodynamic Optimization

Ran computational fluid dynamics simulations in OpenFOAM to analyze flow over the hull, identify drag sources, and refine the chassis geometry for stability and speed.

Results

  • Ranked 4th out of 9,000+ teams worldwide
  • Won the Innovation Award for best ROV design
  • Achieved stable, controllable underwater performance across all task runs
